How you get in

Press rewind.

An unmarked staircase on Davis Street leads down to a storage room of nostalgic relics. At its center sits a 1980s boombox. Press the button marked REWIND and a concealed door opens.

Behind it is a cocktail lounge anchored by Asian artwork, traditional instruments and hand-carved murals. Beyond the lounge, large wooden doors open onto a red corridor of karaoke rooms, each one a different moment in music.

How long can I book a private event for?

A buyout generally requires a minimum of 2 hours. Karaoke rooms are booked by the hour, with a flat minimum on Friday and Saturday nights after 10pm.

Can I customize the menu or the experience?

Yes. The team can customize the food and drinks menu on request, and can do buffet-style or cocktail-hour formats in place of a sit-down dinner.

What's the deposit policy?

A 50% deposit is due on contract signing.

What's the gratuity policy?

All pricing quoted excludes sales tax and a 20% service charge.

Can we bring a cake?

Yes — cake plating is $25 per cake, and birthday decorations can be added for $88.

What if I don't see a room or duration I want?

Use the contact form below and the team will look at availability for you directly.
