What we found on rewindnyc.com

Things guests and Google notice

•The Karaoke page is 3 MB, because its photos are pasted into the page code. It's slow, especially on phones.
•No page has a main heading, and every page shares one title, so Karaoke, Lounge and Private Events can't rank for different searches.
•None of the 20 Karaoke photos have alt text, and the menu is a Canva link (rewindnyc.com/menu is a 404).
•Hero videos are 540p, which looks soft on laptops.
•Private events, your biggest bookings, sit behind a small link at the bottom of the reservations popup, and "Email us" only opens a mail app.

What we did in the concept

✓Lounge and Karaoke side by side on the first screen, with your footage AI-upscaled and your koi and bird animations.
✓A three-step reservation drawer that hands off to SevenRooms prefilled, plus a Book button on every room and a Reserve bar on phones.
✓Private events up front: the three spaces (30 / 70 / 100 guests), your key terms, and your inquiry form with validation.
✓Named rooms and clear rate cards, with capacity and starting prices.
✓A Food & Drink section: pick one of the Five Elements to see its cocktail, and swipe through Chef Lee's dishes.
✓A contact form in place of the mailto link, set in your own Josefin Sans.

What we'd do in the full build

→Roll the design across every page: Home, Lounge, Karaoke, Private Events, Menu, Our Story, FAQ.
→SevenRooms embedded and styled to match; the inquiry form wired to your events inbox with an instant auto-reply and lead tracking.
→SEO: faster pages, proper headings and titles, a real menu page, a Private Events page for "corporate event space Long Island City" and "private karaoke NYC," plus Google Business Profile and event-venue listings.
→Ideas worth discussing: a Chinese-language page for your RedNote audience, an events calendar for Between Sips, and corporate packages with starting prices.
